Emberfall Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

Emberfall is a live wallpaper for Android made by Lanternline Software LLC. This policy explains what the app does with information. Emberfall has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and Lanternline Software LLC runs no server for the app. We do not receive your location, your purchases or anything else about you from the app.

Your location

Sun times come from your phone's time zone unless you choose "Your location" in Settings. Only then does Emberfall ask for Android's approximate location permission. The approximate position is used on your phone to work out sunrise and sunset. It is stored only on your phone, is never sent to us or anyone else by Emberfall, and is left out of Android backups. You can switch to another sun source in Settings or remove the permission in Android settings at any time.

Settings stored on your phone

Emberfall stores your chosen scene and settings on your phone. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may back up the scene choice and settings and restore them to a new phone. Location, purchase status, trial status and download state are excluded from that backup.

Purchases

Emberfall Plus is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Plus and keeps that answer on your phone so Plus works offline. It also keeps a record on your phone of which 24-hour trials you have used. Google's handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Downloads

Full-quality scenes are delivered by Google Play. Google Play receives the technical information it needs to deliver them, as it does for any app download.

Google libraries

Emberfall includes Google's Play Billing and Play Asset Delivery libraries. They communicate with Google Play and may send Google technical and diagnostic information needed to run and protect those services. Emberfall does not add to or read that information.

Crash and performance information

If you allow it on your device, Google Play and Android may share crash and performance statistics with developers through Android vitals. These are provided by Google in aggregate form, and the app contains no crash-reporting service of its own.

Children

Emberfall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

Because we do not hold personal information from the app, there is nothing for us to access or delete. Uninstalling Emberfall removes its data from your phone. To manage or cancel a subscription, or to request a refund, use Google Play.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
